About Me

Hi, my name is Grace McBroom. I am a sophomore graphic design student at the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire with a passion for creativity, art, and visual communication. I enjoy trying new creative things and experimenting with styles to bring ideas to life through my work. Outside of school, I enjoy watching sports, specifically softball, gymnastics, and hockey, staying active, doing nails, and spending time with friends. In the future, I hope to combine my love for creativity, social media, and sports to pursue a career in content or social media marketing/advertisement. My goal is to improve my design and social media skills to continue to grow as a designer and create work that is both visually engaging and meaningful.

Work Experience

I have two years of experience in customer service at the YMCA front desk and spent one year working at Iowa Nutty, serving food and drinks to Iowa Hawkeye fans at football and basketball games. This past summer, I worked at the Veterans Memorial Community Center (VMCC) in both front desk services and childcare. I currently work as a softball coach for the Minnesota Fastpitch Academy and at the YMCA Sports Center as a facility attendant and gymnastics team coach.

Education

I graduated high school in May of 2025 from North Senior High School in North St. Paul, MN. My freshman year of college, I attended Clarke University in Dubuque, IA, for graphic design and to play softball. Due to my major being cut at Clarke, I transferred to the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire to continue my graphic design major.

Achievements

Through my athletic, academic, and artistic experiences, I have earned several achievements. I received All-Conference Honorable Mention in gymnastics for two years in a row, while also being team captain for three years. As an artist, I have received multiple awards for my paintings, including the Scholastic Art and Writing Honorable Mention Award. I also have a drawing piece that was published in the 16th volume of the Tenth Muse Literary Magazine.
